My name is Abby Lorch

Abby Lorch is a 12-year-old performer from North Liberty, Iowa, with a passion for the performing arts. As a dedicated actor, violinist, singer, and dancer, she enjoys every opportunity to grow as a performer and share her love of the arts with others. Abby was honored to become an Iowa State Fair champion for her violin performance and portrayed the Fiddler in Fiddler on the Roof with Revival Theatre Company in Cedar Rapids. She has also appeared in Honk! and enjoys bringing characters to life on stage while taking on new creative challenges.

As a violinist and vocalist, Abby has given numerous performances throughout Iowa at community events, festivals, and special programs, including the Iowa City Arts Festival and the Old Threshers Reunion. In addition to theater and music, Abby is a hardworking and talented dancer who is always striving to become stronger and improve her skills. Whether she is acting, dancing, singing, or playing the violin, Abby approaches every opportunity with enthusiasm, dedication, and a love for performing. She looks forward to continuing to grow as an artist while sharing her passion for the performing arts with others.
